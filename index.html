Gaurav

Owner, second generation

My grandfather Chanchal Lal started this shop in 1994 with two racks and one wooden measuring stick. I still use that stick when a customer wants a length checked twice.

I was ten when I first folded a saree behind this counter. My father used to say, "cloth is trust, the customer cannot test it at home." That line is written on the back wall today.

We do not push. Sit, drink chai, touch the cloth, take your time.

  • Fabric shown in daylight at the door, not under shop lights
  • Measured with a metal tape and cut in front of you
  • Rates written on the tag, no different price for different faces
  • Colour or quality problem? Exchange within 7 days with the bill
  • Free folding, pressing and wrapping for gifts
